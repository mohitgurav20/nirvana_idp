Three Sensors. Infinite Insights.

Bio-Sync integrates three clinical-grade sensors that transmit biometric data every 2 seconds. This real-time fusion creates a comprehensive picture of your physiological state.

Cardio Rhythm

MAX30102 Optical Sensor

Measures real-time heart rate variability using an optical pulse sensor placed on the wrist or chest. Heart rate reflects your sympathetic nervous system activation (stress) and parasympathetic dominance (recovery). Baseline ranges 60–100 BPM.

Physiological Stress Index

Your three biometric inputs feed into a real-time linear regression model running on our Flask backend. The model calculates your Physiological Stress Index (0–100 scale) every 2 seconds using a trained AI algorithm.

Open Source Hardware Integration

Bio-Sync is engineered entirely on medical-grade open hardware modules, giving students and researchers full architectural control to audit and build physiological systems.

Integrates dynamic 240MHz dual-core processing speed with low-power Wi-Fi pathways to transmit telemetry biometrics every 2 seconds without high energy draw.

Clinical Biometrics
